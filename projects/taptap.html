CASE STUDY

TapTap Multimodal Ad Review System

Turning a vague AI-review opportunity into a deployable multimodal moderation workflow for high-volume advertising content.

Enterprise AIMultimodalToB Delivery
OVERVIEW

What this project was about

A multimodal advertising review workflow combining video, audio, OCR and specialized agents. The focus was not only model accuracy, but also latency, cost, operational fit and customer acceptance.

CHALLENGE

What made it difficult

The customer needed faster review at scale while keeping quality high. Video content introduced multiple modalities, inconsistent rule interpretation and a difficult trade-off between accuracy, response time and cost.

SOLUTION

How I approached it

I helped structure the workflow from whole-video review into a modular pipeline: frame extraction and OCR, audio analysis, video slicing, specialized tools and LLM-based risk judgement. The solution evolved through multiple iterations as rules became more detailed and cost pressure increased.

Video / Image→Frame extraction + OCR→Audio / clip analysis→Specialized agents→LLM judgement→Risk output
MY CONTRIBUTION

What I owned

I participated from early requirement discovery through workflow design, customer communication, rule alignment, PoC iteration and cross-functional delivery with engineering, algorithm, operations and legal stakeholders.

IMPACT

What changed

99.2%optimized workflow accuracy
+50%review efficiency
−40%cost
