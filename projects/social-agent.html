CASE STUDY

Enterprise Social Intelligence Agent

An enterprise monitoring agent for tracking social signals and turning fragmented information into structured risk insights.

AgentEnterprise AICost Optimization
OVERVIEW

What this project was about

The product supports enterprise users who create accounts and monitor external risks around their own products or brands. It combines search/monitoring logic with LLM-based synthesis and structured outputs.

CHALLENGE

What made it difficult

The challenge was to make the system useful at enterprise scale without allowing context windows and repeated model calls to grow costs too quickly.

SOLUTION

How I approached it

I worked on workflow and prompt optimization, reducing redundant context and reorganizing model calls so the agent could preserve useful evidence while lowering token usage.

Monitoring target→Source collection→Filtering→LLM analysis→Risk synthesis→Enterprise output
MY CONTRIBUTION

What I owned

I contributed to product logic, model-call design and cost optimization, focusing on how model capability becomes a repeatable enterprise workflow rather than a one-off demo.
